SUMMER WONDERLAND

Tiny Landscape, Big Adventure: Exploring Shaders and 3D Storytelling with Three.js

Real-Time Rendering Meets Handcrafted Fantasy

I created this real-time 3D scene as a personal exploration into storytelling through interactive environments. Using Blender for modeling and scene composition, I built a whimsical landscape featuring a cozy tent, cartoonish rocks, leafy trees, and a mischievous fox on a little adventure. The scene was then brought to life in Three.js, where I used custom GLSL shaders to give the portal a dynamic, playful energy and to scatter flickering fireflies throughout the landscape. This project was a chance to experiment with stylization, animation, and shader effects, while refining my skills in web-based rendering and immersive visual design.

Curious about the process?

Building the Scene in Blender
I started by creating and assembling low-poly assets — including the terrain, fences, trees, split logs, a tiny axe, rocks, stylized lighting posts, and a circular portal built from duplicated bricks. These assets were carefully arranged to support both visual storytelling and modeling efficiency.

To ensure smooth performance in the browser, I prepared the scene for light baking — a technique where complex lighting (like global illumination and soft shadows from ray tracing) is pre-rendered into textures. These baked textures are later applied in Three.js to emulate rich lighting effects without heavy computation.
